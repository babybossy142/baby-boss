Last updated: October 29, 2026

Before publication: Verify this policy reflects your real hosting, analytics, account connection, retention, and support practices. Add a valid contact address on the Contact page.

1. Who we are

Kids Learning Video Automation is an educational animation project. This policy describes the intended handling of personal information in connection with this informational website and any future authorized social publishing features.

2. Information we may process

When visitors browse the site, our hosting provider may process technical information such as IP address, browser type, and request logs. If you contact us, we may receive the information you voluntarily provide. If TikTok account connection is later enabled, authorized integration data may include a basic profile identifier, access tokens, and video publishing status, depending on the permissions granted.

3. How information is used

Information may be used to operate and secure the site, respond to inquiries, authenticate connected accounts, and upload or publish videos only as authorized. We do not claim to collect TikTok account information through this static website today.

4. TikTok authorization

Any future TikTok connection will use an official authorization flow. We will request only necessary scopes and use information solely for the stated integration functions. Access may be revoked through TikTok account settings or by contacting the operator. Tokens must be stored securely by the integration provider and must not be placed in public website files.

5. Sharing and processors

Website hosting may be provided by GitHub Pages. If enabled, video workflows may use authorized services such as Composio, AI media providers, and TikTok. Each service has its own privacy terms. We do not sell personal information.

6. Retention and security

Information should be kept only as long as needed for its stated purpose, account operation, legal obligations, or security. Reasonable technical and organizational safeguards should be used, although no system can guarantee absolute security. Exact retention periods for future integrations will be documented before activation.

7. Children's privacy

Our content is intended to be family-friendly, but account management and integration features are intended for adults. This website does not intentionally request personal information from children. If a parent or guardian believes a child has submitted personal information, please contact us so it can be addressed.

8. Your choices and rights

Depending on applicable law, you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information. You may also revoke a connected TikTok application's access in TikTok settings. Requests can be submitted using the contact details below.

9. Updates

We may update this policy when our practices or integrations change. The latest version and effective date will appear on this page.

10. Contact

For privacy inquiries, see our Contact page.
