About the project

Creative learning, made with care

Kids Learning Video Automation is a creative project for producing original, age-appropriate animated educational content. Our goal is to make simple learning topics engaging through colorful visuals, clear narration, and positive storytelling.

Our production workflow may use AI-assisted tools to prepare scripts, visuals, narration, and editing. The website showcases the project and provides information about how authorized account owners may connect TikTok to a publishing workflow. It does not itself connect to TikTok or publish videos.
